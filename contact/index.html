Before You Call

Stop the source

Shut off the main water valve if a pipe or appliance line is leaking.

Stay safe

Do not enter standing water near outlets or the panel. Call 911 or the utility for any electrical or gas danger first.

Note what you see

Which rooms are wet, what the water came from, and how long it has been there.

Take photos

Photos and video before cleanup help with an insurance claim.
