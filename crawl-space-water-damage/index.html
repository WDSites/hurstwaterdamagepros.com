Emergency Crawl Space Water Damage in Hurst, TX

Standing water under a house soaks insulation, joists and the floor above. Larger water events are covered on the flood damage restoration page.

☎ Call (817) 623-9267

Water in a crawl space is easy to miss because nobody looks under the house until a floor feels soft or a musty smell drifts up. By then the insulation, joists and subfloor may have been wet for days. If you see water under the house, call (817) 623-9267 at any hour, because the line is answered 24/7.

How common are crawl spaces here?

We cannot give you a Hurst figure. No source we trust says how many local homes have crawl spaces. A home on a concrete slab has no crawl space at all, while pier-and-beam houses and some additions do.

If your home is on a slab, the page you want is slab leak water damage. If you are not sure what is under your house, a quick look at the access panel or a call to us will sort it out.

Where the water comes from

  • A broken or leaking supply line, drain line or fitting under the floor
  • A sprinkler line or irrigation backflow assembly that failed and flooded the soil
  • Storm runoff that flows toward the house and enters through vents or low spots
  • A downspout dumping water beside the foundation
  • A leaking air conditioner condensate line or water heater drain

The City of Hurst storm water page suggests pointing downspouts onto the lawn, away from paved areas. The USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service gives similar advice, saying to keep downspouts and surface drainage away from the foundation and sprinkler lines away from the house. For a homeowner, that means a simple downspout extension can matter more than anything done under the floor.

Safety before anyone goes under

Do not crawl into a space with standing water. Wiring, ductwork and gas lines may run through it, and the space is dark, tight and hard to leave in a hurry. Close the main water valve if a pipe is leaking, and cut power to the area only if you can reach the breaker while standing dry.

Call 911 for fire, a gas smell or anyone in danger. Call the City of Hurst Public Works dispatch line at 817-788-7212 only when the problem is on the city side of the meter. If sewage may be involved, stay out and tell us when you call.

How we dry a crawl space

  1. Stop the sourceA plumber, irrigation technician or drainage contractor must stop the water first, since pumping a space that keeps refilling gets nowhere.
  2. Pump out standing waterSubmersible pumps and extraction units remove water from low spots. Crews wear protective gear.
  3. Remove wet materialSoaked insulation is heavy and holds water against the floor, so it comes out. Torn or soiled vapor barrier is bagged and replaced as needed.
  4. Measure the woodMoisture meters read joists, beams and the underside of the subfloor so we know how deep the water went.
  5. Contain and dryPlastic containment keeps wet crawl space air from moving into the house. Dehumidifiers and air movers then dry the wood and the floor above.
  6. Treat and recheckAntimicrobial treatment goes on when the water category calls for it, and daily readings show when the wood is dry.

We do not regrade yards, install drains or fix pipes. A licensed plumber repairs supply and drain lines, and a drainage contractor corrects grading and runoff. We handle the water damage and drying, and our water damage repair service rebuilds finishes above, such as flooring.

Backflow, sprinklers and watering rules

One pattern we hear about is a broken irrigation backflow assembly that floods the soil beside the foundation. The City of Hurst Environmental Services Division lists a Cross Connection Control program for backflow, so the city treats these devices as something to track. Ask the city about your assembly before replacing or moving it.

The city also has year-round watering rules, including no sprinkler watering from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. Check the city's current schedule, because rules can change. Over-watering beside a foundation can feed crawl space moisture, so a shorter run time is cheap prevention.

Mold, standards and what changes the cost

Wet wood and insulation can grow mold when they stay damp. Water damage work follows ANSI/IICRC S500, and homeowners can ask any company whether its crews work to it. If mold is found, testing and cleanup follow ANSI/IICRC S520, and Texas licenses mold assessors and remediators separately, so verify a license on the TDLR search.

Insurance and prevention

The Texas Department of Insurance says most policies cover sudden and accidental water damage, not gradual leaks, and home policies do not cover flood. Storm runoff entering a crawl space can be a flood question, so check your policy and ask your insurer. The City of Hurst floodplain page notes that FEMA has mapped stream flooding but not localized street and yard flooding, which means your yard can flood without a mapped flood zone.
