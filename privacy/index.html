Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you call us or visit this website.

This policy explains what information Hurst Water Damage Pros collects when you call or visit hurstwaterdamagepros.com. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Information You Give Us by Phone

When you call, you may share your name, phone number, address, and a description of the water or mold problem. We use it to understand the job, arrange service, and follow up on the call. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Information From This Website

This website has no forms and does not ask you to enter personal details. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log technical data such as browser type, device, and pages visited to keep the site secure and working.

How We Use Information

  • To answer your call and arrange service
  • To improve the quality of our phone service and train dispatchers
  • To keep the website secure and working
  • To meet legal obligations

Sharing

We share job details with the restoration professionals dispatched to your address so they can do the work. We do not sell your personal information.

Your Choices and Contact

You can ask us not to contact you again or ask what we hold about your call by phoning (817) 623-9267 or emailing contact@hurstwaterdamagepros.com. Our address is 227 NE Loop 820, Hurst, TX 76053.

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always on this page.
