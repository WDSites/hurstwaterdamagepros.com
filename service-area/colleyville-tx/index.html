Little Bear Creek, Big Bear Creek and your street

If you must leave, avoid driving into that crossing or any covered road, and call us once you are safe and the water has begun to enter the house.

Big Bear Creek is crossed by the Pleasant Run Rd bridge. Homes in low spots near a creek may see water at doors or in garages first, so move vehicles and valuables early when storms are forecast. Our flood damage restoration service covers extraction, drying and cleanup after outside water enters.

City water, TRA and who to call first

Colleyville provides its own water, wastewater and garbage service. It buys treated water from the Trinity River Authority, and its wastewater goes to a TRA treatment plant. Before we arrive: if water is still spreading

Find and close the valve

Shut the fixture valve or the main water valve. Texas Department of Insurance advises knowing both indoor and outdoor shutoffs.

Cut power to wet rooms

Switch off those breakers only if the panel is dry and you can reach it standing on a dry floor.

Move valuables up

Take art, electronics, rugs and papers to a dry floor. Lift furniture legs off wet hardwood.

Stay clear of dirty water

Keep people and pets away from water that may include sewage or creek runoff.
