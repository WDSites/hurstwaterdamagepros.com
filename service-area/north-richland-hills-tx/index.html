What aging plumbing, heaters and roofs mean for water damage

With that many houses at 40 years or more, a few failures come up again and again. A supply line that has corroded can weep for weeks behind a wall. A tank water heater that finally gives out can put a full tank across a utility room floor. Roof leaks soak attic insulation first and show up on ceilings later.

We cannot tell you how old your own house is from this page, and the city statistic is a citywide figure. It does tell you where to look. Check under sinks, around the water heater and along attic ceilings now, because slow leaks are the ones insurers often treat as gradual.

Electrical and sewage hazards

If water has reached outlets, light fixtures or the breaker panel, do not touch switches or step into the water. Have a licensed electrician shut off power before anyone goes in.

Water from a sewer backup or toilet overflow carries contamination. Keep children and pets out, avoid contact with skin, and wait for professionals to handle it.

Floodplain facts for North Richland Hills homeowners

The city site has Floodplain Management, Floodplain Determination and Floodplain FAQ pages, and they are the right place to look up an address. The FAQ says property in the 1 percent chance floodplain has about a 26 percent chance of flooding over a 30-year mortgage. It also says over 20 percent of National Flood Insurance Program claims come from outside the floodplain.

The practical lesson is that a home outside a mapped zone can still take on water. Storm water in the city ends up in the Trinity River, and the city says systems can be overwhelmed in heavy rain. Check the FEMA flood map for your address, then ask your insurer about flood coverage, since standard home policies exclude flood damage.

Who handles sewer and water lines in the city

North Richland Hills runs its own water and sewer, and the water office and Building Inspection are at 4301 City Point Dr. Sewer treatment is bought from both Fort Worth and the Trinity River Authority. If sewage is surfacing in the street or at a city cleanout, call the city. If it is coming up inside your house, call us.

Before we arrive: if water is still spreading

Shut the water off

Turn the valve at the fixture or the main valve at the house. Water heater leaks need the cold supply valve above the tank closed.

Be careful with power

Switch off breakers for wet rooms only if the panel is dry and you are standing on a dry surface.

Clear the floor

Move rugs, books, electronics and small furniture out of the wet area. Take photos first.

Avoid contaminated water

Keep kids and pets away from sewage, storm runoff or anything from a drain.

Ring the 24/7 line

Describe what failed, which rooms are wet and whether water is still moving.

Record the damage

Photograph or video each room and keep damaged items for your adjuster.
