What to Do Before a Crew Arrives

When water or mold is in the house, the first minutes matter. Use this short list while you call.

  • Stop the source if it is safe to do so, starting with the main water valve.
  • Do not touch electrical items or outlets near standing water.
  • Keep children and pets out of water that may be contaminated.
  • Take photos before moving anything.
  • Call 911 first for fire, gas smell, or a person in danger.

Water damage is time sensitive, so the order matters. Drying first protects the structure, and repair comes last because new drywall on a wet wall only hides the problem. If you are not sure which stage you are in, the dispatcher can walk through it with you.
