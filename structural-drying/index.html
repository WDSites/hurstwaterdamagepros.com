Structural Drying and Dehumidification in Hurst, TX

Wet framing, subfloors and wall cavities dried to a measured goal with air movers, dehumidifiers and daily readings. This page covers only the drying phase of water mitigation.

☎ Call (817) 623-9267

Once standing water is gone, most of the moisture is still inside the building materials. Structural drying removes it from framing, subfloors, concrete and wall cavities until the readings say it is safe to close them up. Hurst Water Damage Pros plans that work around the structure in front of us, because no two houses dry the same way.

The Four Things That Make Materials Dry

Drying is evaporation, and evaporation needs four things working together. Water has to be exposed to moving air. The air has to be dry enough to take moisture. Heat speeds the process. And the room needs to be closed so the moisture removed from the walls is not replaced from outside.

Air movers supply the airflow. Dehumidifiers dry the air and carry the water out of the building as liquid. If you only run fans, the moisture leaves the wood and goes into the air, then lands on the next cool surface. That is why drying without dehumidification stalls.

Why a Hardware Store Fan Is Not Enough

A box fan moves air in one direction at one height. It does not reach inside wall cavities, under cabinets or beneath flooring, and it cannot lower humidity. It can also push moisture from a wet room into a dry one.

Professional equipment is placed by measurement. Air movers are set to sweep the wet surfaces, dehumidifiers are sized to the volume and the amount of water, and readings tell us whether the plan is working. If a reading stops falling, we change the equipment and not just wait.

Safety before equipment goes in: what to do before a crew arrives

Shut off the water source and stay out of rooms where water is near outlets, power strips or lamps. Turn off power to a wet room at the panel only if the panel is dry and you can reach it safely. Call 911 for fire, a gas smell or anyone in danger.

How the Drying Phase Runs

  1. Set reference readingsWe take meter readings in a dry part of the home to set the drying goal. Wet materials are compared to that reference, not to a general number.
  2. Open and containToe kicks come off, baseboards lift and small holes may be made to reach cavities. Plastic containment keeps the wet zone closed so dehumidifiers work efficiently.
  3. Place equipmentAir movers and dehumidifiers go in by measurement. Floor mat systems or injection are used where water is trapped under flooring.
  4. Monitor and adjustA technician returns, records readings at the same marked points, and moves or removes equipment as areas reach the goal.
  5. Confirm dryWhen the readings match the reference, equipment is removed and the numbers are written down.

A thermal imaging camera is sometimes used during checks. It shows surface temperature differences, and wet areas often look cooler. We use it to decide where to measure, and the meter readings are what count.

Drying follows ANSI/IICRC S500, the Fifth Edition 2021, which is the published standard for professional water damage restoration. Homeowners can ask any company whether its crews work to it. The standard supports drying goals and documentation, but your structure decides the plan.

Hurst Weather, Hurst Humidity

National Weather Service Fort Worth says July and August highs are usually in the 90s and often 100 or more, so air conditioners run hard. A closed, cooled house with a wet subfloor is an ideal setting for LGR dehumidifiers. It also means condensate lines and drain pans near air handlers are real leak sources, so check them if floors near the unit feel damp.

The same NWS source says spring and fall are typically the wettest periods, mostly from thunderstorms. That is when roof leaks and wind-driven rain are most likely to reach walls and ceilings. After a storm, check attic insulation and ceiling corners early and call for readings if anything feels cool or soft.

The Texas Department of Insurance says to dry quickly because mold can start within about a day. Wood framing that stays wet that long can start to support growth. If it does, see our mold remediation page, since mold cleanup follows ANSI/IICRC S520.

Census QuickFacts lists Hurst at about 9.96 square miles with 15,763 households. That is a lot of homes close together, with many layouts and building styles. We plan each home on its own readings and not on a standard setup.

Insurance Basics

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered, and gradual leaks often are not. Daily readings, equipment logs and photos help explain what the drying involved. Check your policy and ask your insurer what documents they need.

Mistakes to Avoid

  • Turning equipment off because the room feels dry
  • Opening windows on a humid day to help drying
  • Closing up walls and floors before readings match the reference
  • Using space heaters on wet floors, which can warp wood
